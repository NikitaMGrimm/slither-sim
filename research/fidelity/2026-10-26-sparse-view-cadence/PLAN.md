# Sparse-view cadence: frozen analysis declaration

Frozen before running the reconstruction or viewing conditional outcome columns, 2026-10-26. Existing aggregate results in the question and published reports are already known. This is a retrospective exploratory analysis, not a prospective independent experiment.

Primary inputs: all six completed #111 second-continuation near-server archives, unfiltered; all six valid #144 local captures (0/500 bots, r1-r3). Exclude the four interrupted #111 attempts under their original quarantine. Supporting cohort: every readable official protocol-19 headless archive in the packet-scheduler manifest; preserve unusable files with reasons. Deduplicate by compressed SHA-256. Verify primary identities against published pins/manifests.

Reconstruct causally from the complete prefix, resetting on each connection/setup, with tools/fidelity/slither_protocol.py World. View means recipient cache membership, not geometry or world population. Separate own state from other snakes; count foods/prey in cache. Disable body contraction (render bookkeeping irrelevant). Never extrapolate unobserved changes or identify missing physics ticks.

Primary analysis: consecutive frames within a setup epoch and continuous active own life, excluding setup and life-boundary intervals. Attribute the incoming header gap to the PRECEDING frame's other-snake count: 0, 1-2, 3-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21+. Also report ending-frame counts and no-other-snake-update frames, all-snake-quiet frames, own-only, pong-only, maintenance-only, and entity-quiet frames. A changed snake is one with a spawn/removal, anchor/tail, changed heading/wanted/speed/mass/kills/score; separately count reported snake IDs even if values repeat. Food activity includes listing/birth/eat/clear, prey includes entry/update/removal, irrespective of net cache delta. Own-only is substantive traffic, not empty under our host rules. Empty/header-only, redundant-motion-only and unsolicited pong-like traffic are separate candidate evidence; do not treat ordinary own anchors as missing-host emissions.

Report denominators, server-header gap n/min/p50/p90/p95/p99/max, fraction <=9 ms and >20 ms, receipt p50/p95, sum of observed gaps, mean gap and frames per server second. Also use nonoverlapping 1,000-ms server-time windows wholly within active life; assign bins only if other-snake membership count stays in one bin throughout the window, and report emission rates. Retain first and maximum-gap witnesses, packet families, connection/frame identity, and all long-gap counterexamples in per-frame output. No masking based on results. Zero samples means unavailable.

Decision: support a missing official per-tick emission only if at least two primary official captures each provide >=1,000 active sparse (0 or 1-2 other snakes) intervals and >=10 server seconds of such exposure, sparse p50 <=9 ms and >=80% gaps <=9 ms, AND packet-level evidence identifies recurring emissions our rules would suppress (not merely own state, food/prey, ping replies or maintenance that our host also sends). A claim that all views emit every tick additionally requires no materially populated sparse stratum contradicting it (>100 intervals with p50 >9 ms or <=9-ms fraction <80%). Otherwise no implementation PR: retain measured failures and recommend matched view/activity comparisons, without retroactively relaxing existing gates. Absence of a qualifying sparse cohort is inconclusive, not equivalence. Any inferred fix must be smallest, official-only, with selected fixture and dated note; responsive unchanged.

Execution: offline analysis only, one process; no game-server connections, no capture acquisition. CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2 and <=4 logical CPUs if any build is needed. Keep raw archives and personal/machine/network details outside Git.
